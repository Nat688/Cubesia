Cubesia Minecraft Server

A survival server with stackable spawners, custom enchantments, minions and much more.

Minecraft 1.21.8 - 26.3
Patch Wiki Rules

How to join

  1. Open MinecraftLaunch the game on a supported version (1.21.8 - 26.3).
  2. Add the serverGo to Multiplayer, then Add Server.
  3. Paste the IPEnter cubesia.realistic.host and join.

What you will find

Spawners Stack up to 10,000 spawners, with storage, XP and loot filter. No Silk Touch needed. Enchantments More than 80 custom enchantments for armor, bows, tools, fishing rods and weapons. Minions Automated workers that gather and produce resources and store them in your chests. AFK Zone A random reward for every minute spent there, even while you are away. Graves Your items and XP are kept safe in a grave for 10 minutes after you die. Personal Vaults Private double chests that only you can open, with the /pv command.
