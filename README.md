### Cubesia

Welcome to Cubesia, a survival server where every detail has been crafted to make your adventure smoother, richer and more rewarding. Whether you love building, farming, fighting, trading or simply exploring, there is always something new to discover, and every update brings even more.

**⛏️ SURVIVE, BUILD, PROGRESS**

Level up to 100 and unlock permanent bonuses such as more health, speed, armor, luck and reach. Complete 88 collections across farming, mining, combat, lumberjack and fishing, earn money and experience with 13 jobs, and discover 86 custom advancements added on top of the vanilla ones. Equip one of 34 tags to show off your style in the chat, and climb the leaderboards, which are tracked by day, week, month and year so everyone has a chance to shine. Your profile shows your playtime, kills, deaths and balance, and the settings menu lets you toggle the scoreboard, announcements, payments, trades and fishing messages to play exactly the way you like.

**💰 A LIVING ECONOMY**

Sell your loot with the sell menu and watch your multiplier grow: every category has its own progression, up to x3.0. Trade on the Auction House, hunt rare offers on the Black Market that restocks every hour, post orders for the items you need, place bounties on rivals or trade safely with your friends. Invest your money and earn an income every second while you are online.

Three currencies keep the economy healthy: money, shards and gems. Earn shards by relaxing in the AFK zone, then spend your gems on exclusive perks.

**🏅 GRADES**

Grades are permanent upgrades. Climb the money ladder from Stone to Nether Star, or choose the gem grades Coal, Redstone and Quartz. They unlock more homes, personal vaults, auction slots, orders and player warps, but also special abilities: elevators, infinite flight, random teleport in the Nether and the End, and even changing the mob of a spawner.

**🧟 SPAWNERS, MINIONS & FARMS**

Stack up to 10,000 spawners, store their drops, filter what you want to keep, sell everything in one click and use their experience to repair your gear. No Silk Touch is needed to move them. Put minions to work: farmers, miners, lumberjacks, fishers, slayers, sellers and more will keep working for you, even while you are offline. Harvest and replant crops with a single right-click, and keep lag away thanks to the item stacker and the automatic cleanups.

**📚 80 CUSTOM ENCHANTMENTS**

Armor, bows, tools, fishing rods and weapons all have unique enchantments, from Veinminer and Telekinesis to Dragon Heart and Darkness Cloak. Vanilla enchantments go beyond their usual limits, the anvil never says "Too Expensive" and you can even color your item names. Every enchantment is listed in our wiki with its level, weight and compatible items.

**🎣 FISHING, CRATES & EVENTS**

Catch 99 custom fish across seven rarities, from Common to Unique, apply baits to your rod and compete in fishing contests every two hours. Dig deep during mining competitions, open Common, Amethyst, Sapphire and Crimson crates, vote on seven sites for keys and money, and answer chat games to win rewards. Seasonal events, like our Halloween calendar, bring exclusive gifts. Your fly time can even be won from crates, so keep an eye on the sky.

**🛡️ QUALITY OF LIFE**

Carry animals and chests with CarryOn, open shulker boxes straight from your inventory, use portable crafting tables, anvils and more with simple commands, and never lose your items thanks to graves. Enjoy elevators, night vision, cauldron concrete, nether portals made of crying obsidian, no crop trampling and many small improvements you will love.

**🧭 TRAVEL & HOMES**

Set your homes, create public player warps for your shops and builds, teleport to your friends with /tpa and explore far from spawn with random teleport in the Overworld, the Nether and the End. A dedicated AFK zone rewards you with shards for every minute you spend there.

**⚔️ FAIR PLAY**

Combat tagging keeps PvP honest, bounties add a bit of thrill, and our staff team is always there to help. Not sure where to start? Open the in-game /menu to find every command in one click, with a complete wiki just one page away.

**💬 A COMMUNITY THAT STAYS CONNECTED**

Link your Minecraft account to Discord to chat with the server, receive your roles automatically and claim a welcome reward. Play with friends in teams of up to 30 members, share a chest, set team warps, upgrade your team and grow together. Our Discord is also the place to follow every update, meet other players and share your builds.

Join Cubesia today and start your story!
