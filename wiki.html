Grades

Grades are permanent upgrades that make your life on Cubesia easier: more homes, more vaults, more auction slots, more orders and a few exclusive abilities. Open the grades menu from the /shop to see what you own and what is next.

There are two families of grades, and both of them are bought once and kept forever.

Money grades

Money grades are bought in order with the money you earn in game. Each grade includes every perk of the previous ones, so climbing the ladder always makes you stronger.

Gem grades are purchased with gems and unlock perks that money grades cannot, such as random teleport in the Nether and the End. They are cheaper when you already own the previous gem grade.

Starting perks

Without any grade, every player has 1 vault, 1 home, 2 auction slots, 1 order and 1 player warp. The VIP status is separate and adds /heal, /feed, 2 extra player warps and 18 extra vaults (see VIP only).

Spawners

Spawner GUI

Every Spawner includes its own management interface, giving you quick access to everything related to your mob farm.

The Chest is the main storage, where every item dropped by your mobs is collected automatically.

The Mob Head acts as a convenient shortcut to instantly sell every stored item without opening the storage first.

You will also find the Experience storage, allowing you to collect all accumulated XP whenever you want. This experience can also be used to repair equipment enchanted with Mending, making your farm even more efficient.

Storage

The Storage menu includes a variety of management tools designed to help you organize, filter, collect, sell, and control every item produced by your spawner.

The Cauldron automatically sorts the stored items, making your storage much easier to organize.

The Hopper opens the loot filter, allowing you to enable or disable specific mob drops.

The Gold Ingot instantly sells every item currently stored inside the spawner.

The Bundle transfers all stored items directly into your inventory.

Each storage page holds 45 slots, and the experience storage keeps up to 1,000 XP until you collect it.

Finally, the Dropper drops every stored item onto the ground, or only the current storage page if your storage spans multiple pages.

Loot Filter

The Loot Filter allows you to choose exactly which drops should be stored by your spawner.

It is divided into two categories: Allowed and Disallowed.

Any item moved to the Disallowed category will no longer appear in the spawner's storage, helping you keep only the drops you actually want.

Switching an item between the two categories is as simple as clicking on it.

Items with durability, such as tools, weapons, and armor, are automatically grouped together regardless of their durability value, making the filter easy to configure and maintain.

Stacking

Spawners can be stacked together to save space and increase the efficiency of your mob farms.

To add spawners to an existing stack, simply right-click the spawner. A normal right-click adds one spawner, while Shift + Right-Click adds as many spawners as possible from your inventory in a single action.

To remove spawners from a stack, use Left-Click. A normal left-click removes one spawner, while Shift + Left-Click removes an entire stack at once, making it much faster to relocate or reorganize your farms.

Spawners also work faster as a stack: a spawner produces between 1 and 4 mobs every 25 seconds when a player is within 32 blocks, and a hopper placed under it can collect the drops automatically. Spawners are also protected from explosions.

Each spawner stack can contain up to 10,000 spawners, allowing you to build extremely compact and efficient mob farms while saving valuable space.

Unlike vanilla Minecraft, Silk Touch is not required to break and recover spawners. An iron, golden, diamond or netherite pickaxe is enough: the spawner costs a single point of durability and goes straight into your inventory, so you can move and manage your farms freely.

Changing the mob

You can change the mob of a spawner with a spawn egg: use the spawn egg of the mob you want on the spawner.

This lets you reuse your spawners and adapt your mob farms without having to find new ones.

Changing the mob of a spawner is a perk of the Emerald and Quartz grades (and every grade above them).

Auction House

The Auction House is the player-driven marketplace of the server, where you can sell your items to other players and browse everything that others have put on sale.

Open it at any time with /ah (you can also use /auction, /auctionhouse or /hdv).

You are free to set the price you want for each item, so you decide how much your loot, your farms or your rare finds are worth.

Every listing remains on sale for 7 days, giving other players plenty of time to find it and buy it. Once a listing has expired, you can recover your item from the Expired tab.

To put an item on sale, hold it and use /ah sell <price> [amount]. Prices accept shortcuts such as K (thousand), M (million) and B (billion), so /ah sell 2.5M works as you would expect. The minimum price is 10.

The interface lets you browse by category (Blocks, Weapons, Armor, Tools, Consumables, Resources, Enchanted Books and Miscellaneous), search for a specific item, and review your active listings, your purchases and your sales history.

Each new listing is also announced in the #auctionhouse channel of our Discord, with the price, the item and more details, so you never miss a good deal. Open the channel.

Whether you want to turn your farm surplus into money or find the rare item you have been looking for, the Auction House is the place to do it.

A 10% tax is applied to every sale made on the Auction House. Players with the Diamond grade (or the Quartz gem grade) and above pay no tax at all. The number of items you can list at once also grows with your grade.

Black Market

The Black Market is a shop for vanilla items.

Its stock changes every hour and follows a rarity system, so the offers you see are never quite the same.

Every restock brings 15 offers split by rarity: 5 common, 5 rare, 3 epic and 2 legendary. Rarer offers are scarcer, so do not wait too long when you spot something you like.

Open it at any time with /blackmarket, and come back regularly to see what is on sale.

Trade

The Trade system lets you exchange items directly with another player through a dedicated interface, so you never have to drop anything on the ground and hope for the best.

Use /trade <player> (or /trades) to start a trade with the player of your choice.

The trade window lets both players put forward their items, making every exchange simple, clear and transparent.

The other player has 60 seconds to accept your request, and both sides must confirm the final offer after a short 5-second safety delay. Trades between accounts on the same connection are not allowed, and you can disable incoming requests at any time with /trade toggle.

It is the easiest way to swap resources, share loot with your friends or settle a deal with someone you trust.

Homes

Homes let you save a place and come back to it whenever you want, so you never have to walk home again.

Open the home menu with /home to set, delete and teleport to your homes. The menu shows 5 homes per page.

Every teleport has a 3-second countdown, and it is cancelled if you move, so stand still until it is over.

Every player starts with 1 home. Your grade unlocks more, up to 5 homes with the Nether Star grade.

Random Teleport

Random Teleport sends you to a random spot of the world, ideal to find untouched land for your next base or farm.

Use /rtp to open the menu and pick a world: Overworld, Nether or End. You are teleported within a 50,000-block radius after a 3-second countdown (cancelled if you move), and you must wait 60 seconds before using it again. It is completely free.

The Overworld is open to everyone. The Nether is unlocked by the Coal gem grade and the End by the Redstone gem grade.

Player Warps

Player Warps let every player create a public teleport point, perfect for showing off a build, opening a shop or sharing a farm.

Open the menu with /pw (or /playerwarp, /playerwarps, /pwarp, /pwarps) to browse the warps created by other players, sorted by category: Building, Shop, Farm, PvP, Event, Fun, Special and Other.

Teleporting has a short 3-second countdown, and a warp name can be up to 16 characters long. Creating a warp is currently free.

You start with 1 player warp. Your grade unlocks more, and VIP adds 2 extra warps.

Orders

Orders let you ask other players for the blocks, items and resources you need.

Open the orders interface with /orders (or /order) to create a request or to browse the requests made by other players.

When creating an order, you simply select the item you are looking for in the selection menu, and the request becomes visible to everyone on the server.

You can have one active order by default, and your grade raises this limit.

Other players can then see what is needed and help you out, which is perfect when you are missing a specific resource or when you want to help the community.

A 10% tax is also applied to orders.

Rewards

Rewards give you free goodies just for playing on the server, such as keys and much more.

Open the rewards interface with /rewards (or /reward) to see everything that is available to you.

Some rewards can be claimed every hour (a Common Key), while others are available once per day (250 shards), so there is always a reason to come back and check the menu.

The more regularly you log in, the more rewards you will collect.

Crates

Crates are mystery chests that you open with a matching key. Each crate has its own reward pool, and the rarer the crate, the better the loot.

Keys can be found in many places: voting, rewards, jobs and special events. There are four crates on the server:

  • Common Crate – the entry-level crate, and the one you will open the most.
  • Amethyst Crate – a pool of 5 tool rewards.
  • Sapphire Crate – a pool of 6 high-value rewards.
  • Crimson Crate – the biggest pool, with 15 rewards including armor, weapons, an Elytra, a Mace, a Trident, a Bow, a Crossbow and tools.

The Common Crate is not only about gear: it can also drop 30 minutes of fly time and, with a bit of luck, a key for a better crate.

Teams

Teams let you play together with your friends and share your progress on the server.

Open the team interface with /team (or /teams). Each team comes with its own menu, where you can manage everything in one place:

  • Members – see the full list of players who are part of your team.
  • Settings – customize how your team works.
  • Upgrades – improve your team as it grows.
  • Shared chest – a storage space that every member can access.
  • Team bank – a shared balance for the whole team.

A team starts with up to 20 members and can grow to 30 through upgrades. Team names can be up to 12 characters long and tags up to 4. Invitations expire after 5 minutes, and your team can set up to 3 team warps (teleports have a 3-second countdown).

The shared chest starts at 3 rows and can be upgraded to 6. Friendly fire between teammates is enabled by default, but you can change it in the team settings.

Working as a team makes it much easier to organize big projects, share resources and take on the challenges of the server together.

Leaderboards

Leaderboards let you compete against the other players of the server and see exactly where you stand.

Open the leaderboard interface with /leaderboard (or /leaderboards), climb the rankings, outdo your rivals and show everyone that you are among the best players on Cubesia.

Rankings are available for balances, shards, kills, mob kills, deaths, damage dealt, playtime, jumps, blocks mined, fish caught and items crafted. Many boards are tracked per day, week, month and year, so newcomers always have a chance to shine.

You can also check some rankings directly with /money top and /shards top.

Profile

Your profile is your identity card on Cubesia. Open yours with /profile (or /profil) to see your first join date, kills, deaths, playtime, balance and experience at a glance.

Settings

Make the server yours with /settings. Each option is a simple toggle:

  • Scoreboard – show or hide the sidebar.
  • Broadcasts – show or hide automatic server announcements.
  • Payments – accept or refuse money from other players (like /paytoggle).
  • Trades – accept or refuse trade requests.
  • Fish catch messages – the chat message for each fish you catch.
  • Custom fishing – turn the custom fishing system on or off.
  • Fish boss bar – the boss bar shown while fishing.

Levels

Player levels make you stronger over time: each level boosts your stats, such as your health and your strength.

Open the levels interface with /level (or /lvl, /levels) to check your progression.

There are 100 levels to reach. Each level asks for more experience than the last, and every one of them rewards you with money. Along the way you unlock permanent bonuses such as more health (up to 20 hearts), speed, armor, luck and a longer reach.

Looking for more goals? Check out Collections.

They are directly integrated into the advancements menu (the L key by default), so there is nothing extra to open.

Cosmetics

Cosmetics let you customize your experience with extras such as mounts and more.

Open the cosmetics menu at any time with /cosmetics. You will find gadgets, particle effects, pets, morphs, hats, suits, emotes, projectile effects and death effects.

Tags

Tags are small titles displayed next to your name in the chat, a stylish way to show who you are on Cubesia, whether you are a humble Farmer or an unstoppable Overlord.

Open the menu with /tags (or /tag). Click a tag you own to equip it, and use Remove my tag to go back to the default prefix.

Tags you do not own yet appear locked. You can buy them in the Cosmetic Shop (Buy tags), with prices starting at 100,000. There are 34 tags in five rarities:

Chat Games

Chat Games are small games that appear regularly in the chat to bring a little extra fun to the server.

They come in many forms, such as trivia questions, math equations and many other challenges.

A new game starts roughly every 8 to 10 minutes, and you have 45 seconds to answer. The games include Math, Trivia, Unscramble, Type, Unreverse, Completion and Guess The Number.

Keep an eye on the chat and be the first to type the correct answer to win the round and a money reward.

Server Changelog

The server changelog is available directly in game, so you can follow every update without leaving Minecraft.

Use the /changelog command to read it at any time.

It lists the changes of each patch (new features, fixes, balance changes and removals) using the same colors as the Patch page of this website.

Discord

Cubesia and the Discord server are directly connected, so you can stay with your friends even when you are not in game.

Linking your account

Link your Minecraft account to your Discord account to unlock the chat bridge features, receive your roles automatically and earn a one-time reward of 25,000.

How to link your account: run /link in Minecraft to get a 4-character code, then send /link <code> on Discord. The code expires after 5 minutes. To remove the link, use /unlink.

Chat bridge

A Discord bot mirrors the in-game chat in a dedicated channel and sends your Discord messages back to the server, so conversations never stop. The bot can also show your profile (link status, kills) and the top kills leaderboard directly on Discord.

Role synchronization

Once your accounts are linked, your in-game roles are mirrored on Discord, and the reverse is also true. This includes the community and staff roles such as VIP, MVP, Friends, Beta Tester, Helper, Moderator, Senior Moderator, Admin, Dev and Owner.

Shop grades are not synchronized with Discord roles.

You can find the invitation any time with /discord.

Death Messages

The server now features new, custom death messages.

Whenever a player dies, a redesigned message is displayed in the chat, making every death a little more memorable.

Fly Time

Fly time lets you fly around for a limited amount of time, and it can be obtained from the Common Crate.

Once you have some fly time, use /fly to take off whenever you want and explore, build or travel across the server from above.

Open the crate and, with a bit of luck, fly time will be one of your rewards. Your remaining fly time is kept when you die.

The Nether Star grade unlocks infinite flight, and so does the Quartz gem grade. See Grades.

AFKZone

The AFK Zone is a dedicated area designed to reward players even while they are away from their keyboard.

For every minute spent in the AFK Zone, you will receive a random reward. The longer you remain there, the more rewards you will collect, giving you additional chances to obtain valuable resources over time.

You can access the area at any time using the /afk command, which teleports you directly to the AFK Zone.

You are also free to leave the zone whenever you wish to continue exploring, building, trading, fighting, or progressing on the server.

The available rewards and their drop chances are as follows:

  • 15% – 1 Shard
  • 20% – 3 Shards
  • 30% – 5 Shards
  • 20% – 10 Shards
  • 11% – 25 Shards
  • 3% – 100 Shards
  • 1% - 500 Shards

The AFK Zone is a simple way to earn extra rewards while taking a break, making every minute spent online a little more rewarding.

Graves

A grave system is available to protect your items and experience for 10 minutes after death.

Even if you die in lava, your belongings will remain safely stored inside your grave, giving you enough time to return and recover them.

Simply travel back to the location of your death to retrieve everything. Keep in mind that you'll have to make the journey on foot (unless you're using a Handic Card 😄).

When you die, a grave is automatically created at the exact location of your death, and its coordinates are immediately displayed so you can easily find your way back.

Once you reach your grave, you can right-click it to retrieve your items one by one, or use Shift + Right-Click to collect everything instantly.

You can also use the /grave list command to manage your graves. It displays all of your active graves, their locations, and the remaining time before each one disappears, helping you recover your belongings before they are lost forever.

You keep 100% of your experience, and every grave disappears after 10 minutes.

A grave can be opened by any player, not only its owner. Return quickly, or stay close to your teammates!

This system provides an extra layer of protection for your valuables, making death much less punishing while still encouraging you to return and recover your equipment.

SellWands

Sell Wands allow you to sell the contents of an entire chest instantly with just a single click, making resource management much faster and more convenient.

Each Sell Wand has its own multiplier, which depends on its type and directly increases the amount of money you receive from each sale.

They also have a limited durability that decreases with every use, with higher rarity Sell Wands generally offering both better multipliers and more durability.

To use a Sell Wand, simply left-click a chest to inspect its contents and view the total number of items along with the amount of money you would earn from selling them.

If you are satisfied with the result, simply right-click the chest to sell all eligible items instantly and receive your money immediately.

This system allows you to manage large quantities of items efficiently while maximizing your profits, making Sell Wands an essential tool for farms and automated resource production.

Vaults

Personal Vaults (PVs) are secure private double chests that can only be accessed by their owner, providing a safe place to store your most valuable items.

You can store virtually any type of item inside, including valuable resources, rare equipment, and long-term supplies, while stackable items behave just like they do in normal chests to maximize storage efficiency.

Use the /pv command (or /vault) to open your Personal Vault menu at any time and access your stored items from anywhere on the server.

Inside the /pv menu, you can Shift + Click on a vault to customize its icon, replacing the default barrel with an item of your choice for a cleaner and more personalized interface.

Every player starts with 1 Personal Vault. The VIP perk adds 18 more, and every grade unlocks additional vaults, up to 27 with the Nether Star grade.

Fish

The server features a complete custom fishing system with dozens of unique fish represented by custom player heads, each with its own rarity, value, and collection progress.

Every fish you catch is automatically recorded in your personal collection, encouraging exploration and giving you new species to discover across the world.

The main command is /fish, which opens the fishing menu and provides access to every available feature, including bait management, your collection, statistics, and more.

Use /fish journal to browse your Fishing Journal, where you can view every available fish, track your discoveries, and monitor your overall completion progress.

Fish can be sold instantly with /sellfish, allowing you to quickly convert your catches into money without needing to visit a shop.

Use /fish sellall to sell all your fish at once, and /fish toggle to enable or disable custom fishing.

If you have purchased or obtained fishing baits, use /fish applybaits to apply them to your fishing rod and benefit from their special effects while fishing.

During fishing competitions and server events, /fish top displays the live leaderboard, allowing you to see the current rankings and track who is leading the event in real time.

There are 99 fish to discover, spread across seven rarities. The rarer the fish, the more it is worth:

Fishing competitions start every 2 hours and last 10 minutes. The top 3 win money and experience, so grab your rod when the announcement appears!

Baits make your catches even better, and up to 10 can be applied to a single rod. Treasure Hunter boosts your chances of finding treasure, Rarity Upgrade only lets Epic and rarer fish bite, and Shark Hunter and Whale Hunter target the biggest creatures of the sea. Most baits can also be crafted.

Whether you enjoy collecting every species, earning money through fishing, or competing against other players during events, the custom fishing system offers plenty of progression and rewards for every type of fisherman.

Mining Competition

Mining competitions are server events in the same spirit as the fishing contests, but made for miners.

Compete against the other players while you mine, and use /miningcomp to follow the current competition.

A new competition starts every 30 minutes. In Stone Rush (5 minutes) you break as many blocks as you can, while Ore Hunt (10 minutes) rewards the best ore collectors. The top 5 players of each round win a money reward.

Elevator

Elevators provide a fast and convenient way to travel vertically through your base without using ladders or stairs.

To create an elevator, simply throw an Ender Pearl onto a wool block. Then, place another elevator block above and/or below it to create a connected elevator system.

Once both elevator blocks are in place, simply jump while standing on the lower elevator to travel upward, or sneak on the upper elevator to travel downward instantly.

Both elevator blocks must be the same wool color in order to connect and function correctly.

Elevator blocks come in all 16 wool colors, so you can build several independent elevators side by side.

Creating elevators is unlocked by the Iron grade.

This system allows you to move quickly between different floors while keeping your builds clean, compact, and easy to navigate.

CarryOn

CarryOn lets you pick up and move almost anything with your own hands: animals, villagers, chests and plenty of other blocks, without breaking them and without losing their contents.

To pick something up, sneak and right-click it with both hands empty. To put it down, simply right-click on the block where you want it. You can only reach targets within 3 blocks.

It works on all entities except bosses and on many blocks such as chests, with the notable exception of spawners, which have their own system (see Spawners).

  • Entities – hostile mobs can be carried too. Pets can only be picked up by their owner. Very large mobs (such as the Ender Dragon, Wither, Warden, Elder Guardian or Ghast) cannot be carried.
  • Stacking animals – sneak and right-click an animal while carrying another one to stack up to 10 animals on your back.
  • Blocks – chests keep their items. Beds, doors, portals and other fragile blocks cannot be carried.

Carrying something is not free: you are slowed down, your hotbar slot is locked and you cannot attack. If you die while carrying, what you hold is put down on the ground.

CarryOn respects claims and protections. You cannot pick up something you are not allowed to touch.

Jobs

Jobs allow you to earn both money and experience by performing everyday activities such as mining, farming, fishing, building, hunting, and much more.

Use the /jobs command (or /job, /skills, /skill) to open the Jobs menu, where you can browse every available profession and manage your progress.

Right-click on a job to open its detailed information page, showing how much experience and money you earn for each supported action, helping you choose the most profitable activities.

Left-click on a job to view its leaderboard, allowing you to compare your progress with other players and see who has reached the highest level in each profession.

There are 13 jobs: Hunter, Miner, Farmer, Husbandry, Fisher, Witcher, Lumberjack, Digger, Gladiator, Guardian, Producer, Trader and Traveler. Every level-up grants you money and a Common Key.

Whether you focus on a single profession or level up multiple jobs, the system provides a rewarding way to progress while playing normally on the server.

Bounty

The Bounty system allows players to place a cash reward on another player's head, encouraging PvP and creating exciting hunting opportunities across the server.

If a player with an active bounty is killed, the player who eliminates them automatically receives the bounty reward.

Use /bounty to open the bounty menu and see every active bounty on the server.

To place a bounty, simply use the command /bounty add <player> <amount>, replacing <player> with the target's username and <amount> with the amount of money you wish to offer.

The minimum bounty is 100. To keep things fair, you cannot claim a bounty from a player on your own connection or in your own team, and a bounty is kept if its target dies naturally.

Whether you want revenge, create friendly competition, or simply make PvP more rewarding, the Bounty system adds an extra layer of excitement to every encounter.

Combat Tag

Combat Tag keeps PvP fair: once you hit or are hit by another player, you are in combat for 30 seconds.

  • If you disconnect during combat, you are killed and your items drop.
  • Most commands are blocked while you are tagged, so you cannot teleport away mid-fight.
  • You cannot run across a protected border to escape: you are pushed back.

Fight it out, or wait for the timer to end before leaving.

Sell

The /sell command allows you to quickly sell a wide variety of items without needing to visit a physical shop.

Simply open the selling interface with /sell (or /sellgui) and place the items you wish to sell to instantly receive the corresponding amount of money.

Every sellable item has a predefined Worth, which is displayed directly in its tooltip, making it easy to know its value before selling.

If you want to check prices without opening the selling menu, you can also use /sellprice (or /sellprices) to browse the complete list of item values available on the server.

To sell your entire inventory in one go, use /sellall, and use /autosell to enable or disable the automatic selling of the items you selected.

Selling is tax-free, and a single transaction can reach up to 1,000,000. Note that /sellall never sells enchanted items.

This system provides a fast and convenient way to turn your resources into money while helping you plan farms and maximize your profits.

ClearLag

ClearLag keeps the server smooth by regularly cleaning up the world. Every 30 minutes, all dropped items and all unneeded entities are removed, which greatly reduces lag for everyone.

You will be warned beforehand with a countdown (at 60, 10, 3, 2 and 1 seconds), so you have time to pick up what you care about. You can choose how the messages are displayed, or turn them off, using /clearlag togglemessages, /clearlag togglemessage and /clearlag togglesound.

Do not worry about your companions and valuables: many things are protected.

  • Mobs – tamed pets, named mobs, bred animals and mobs you have tagged are never removed. Bosses are also safe.
  • Items – precious drops such as diamond, netherite and emerald gear and blocks, elytra, totems, enchanted golden apples, nether stars, beacons, tridents, dragon eggs and heads, and shulker boxes are protected.
  • Around you – items dropped in the last 20 seconds or within 10 blocks of a player are left alone.
  • Other entities – paintings, end crystals, minecarts, boats and allays are never cleared.

The Item Stacker is part of ClearLag: it merges identical dropped items into stacks and groups XP orbs, so large farms stay light on the server.

Small Changes

A collection of smaller improvements and quality-of-life features that make everyday gameplay smoother. Each one is simple on its own, but together they make a real difference.

Shulkers

Shulker Boxes can now be opened directly from your inventory, without needing to place them on the ground first.

Simply carry a Shulker Box in your inventory to access its contents instantly, saving you the time of placing and breaking it every time you need an item.

This feature makes inventory management much faster, more convenient, and more efficient, especially during mining, exploration, or building sessions.

Cauldron Concrete

Cauldron Concrete makes crafting concrete much faster by allowing you to convert Concrete Powder into regular Concrete without placing each block individually.

Simply hold the desired Concrete Powder, then Shift + Right-Click on a water-filled cauldron to instantly harden it into normal Concrete.

This feature saves a considerable amount of time when building large structures, as you no longer need to place and break every Concrete Powder block manually.

It is a simple but extremely useful quality-of-life improvement for builders and anyone working on large construction projects.

Cauldrons also no longer lose water when converting Concrete Powder into Concrete, so a single filled cauldron can harden as many blocks as you need, with no need to refill it between conversions.

Crying Portal

Nether Portals can also be built using Crying Obsidian, giving you additional flexibility when designing your builds.

A portal made entirely of Crying Obsidian functions exactly like a regular Obsidian portal and can be lit in the same way.

This feature allows you to create unique portal designs while taking advantage of the distinctive appearance and animated texture of Crying Obsidian.

Whether for aesthetics or creativity, Crying Obsidian is a fully valid alternative for constructing Nether Portals on the server.

Axolotl Feed

Axolotl Feed lets you feed axolotls directly using Tropical Fish, without needing to capture them in a water bucket first.

Simply use a Tropical Fish on an axolotl to feed or breed it, making the process much faster and more convenient.

The traditional method using a Bucket of Tropical Fish still works exactly as in vanilla Minecraft, so you are free to use whichever method you prefer.

This quality-of-life feature makes breeding and caring for axolotls much easier while preserving the original game mechanics.

Messages Villager / Trader

Villager & Wandering Trader Notifications keep you informed about important events involving villagers and wandering traders.

Whenever a Villager dies, a server message is automatically sent, including the exact coordinates of where the death occurred, making it easier to investigate or react quickly.

Whenever a Wandering Trader spawns, a notification is also broadcast to all players, letting everyone know that a trader has appeared somewhere in the world.

These notifications improve awareness across the server, helping players protect their villagers and ensuring nobody misses the arrival of a Wandering Trader.

Collectible Blocks

Collectible Blocks improve block collection by allowing certain normally fragile or unobtainable blocks to be recovered more easily.

Glass, all Stained Glass variants, every type of Glass Pane, and Ender Chests will always drop themselves when broken, regardless of the tool you use or whether it has the Silk Touch enchantment.

In addition, Budding Amethyst and Dirt Path can now be collected with a tool enchanted with Silk Touch, allowing players to move and use this valuable block in their own farms and builds.

These changes make building, decorating, and resource management much more convenient while preserving the usefulness of the Silk Touch enchantment for special blocks.

No Trample

No Crop Trample prevents farmland and crops from being destroyed when players or mobs walk, run, or jump across them.

You can safely move through your farms without worrying about accidentally turning farmland back into dirt or damaging fully grown crops.

This protection is especially useful for large farms, public areas, and multiplayer environments where many players or animals regularly pass through cultivated land.

It also makes harvesting and replanting much less frustrating, allowing you to move freely while working on your fields without constantly repairing damaged farmland.

This protection is particularly valuable on multiplayer servers, where players, animals, and even unexpected situations can easily damage farmland. Whether you're harvesting, sprinting through your base, or welcoming visitors to your farms, your crops will remain completely safe from accidental trampling.

You can still break farmland on purpose: simply hold a shovel and jump onto the farmland, and it will break as usual.

Harvest Master

Harvest Master makes farming significantly faster by allowing you to harvest and replant crops with a single action.

Simply right-click on a fully grown crop to harvest it instantly. If you have the required seeds or crop in your inventory, it will be automatically replanted at the same time, keeping your farm ready for the next growth cycle.

This feature works with virtually all harvestable crops, eliminating the need to break each plant and manually place seeds after every harvest.

It is especially useful for large farms, where it saves countless clicks and greatly improves harvesting efficiency while preserving the layout of your fields.

Whether you're farming for food, trading, or resources, Harvest Master lets you collect crops more quickly and spend more time expanding your farm instead of maintaining it.

Item Stacker

Item Stacker is an optimized stacking system that greatly reduces the number of dropped entities on the ground, helping to improve overall server performance.

Instead of creating hundreds or thousands of separate item entities, identical dropped items are automatically combined into stacks of up to 6,400 items within a radius of 3 blocks, and each stack shows its item name and count above it.

This significantly reduces lag during large farms, automatic storage systems, and mass item drops while still allowing players to collect the entire stack normally.

By minimizing the number of entities loaded in the world, the Item Stacker system keeps gameplay smoother and the server more stable, even during periods of heavy activity.

Night Vision

Exploring caves or mining at night is much easier with /nightvision, which toggles night vision on and off whenever you want.

Mining, building underground and exploring the ocean floor no longer require torches or potions: simply run the command and see clearly.

Enchantment Table

The Enchantment Table now keeps your Lapis Lazuli in place: when you close the interface, the lapis lazuli remains inside the table instead of being given back to you.

This means you no longer have to place your lapis lazuli again every time you reopen the table, which makes enchanting several items in a row much faster and less tedious.

It is a small change, but a very welcome one for anyone who enchants a lot of equipment.

Bees

Bees no longer attack once their nest or beehive has been destroyed.

Breaking or moving a nest or a beehive is now much safer: the bees that lived there stop being a threat as soon as their habitat is gone, so you do not have to run away from an angry swarm.

This makes it easier to relocate hives, collect honey and set up bee farms without worrying about bees chasing you.

Bone Meal

Bone Meal can now be used on flowers, cactus and sugar cane, in addition to the plants it already works on in vanilla Minecraft.

Simply use Bone Meal on one of them to speed up its growth, which is especially handy to get your cactus and sugar cane farms started.

Whether you are building a large farm or simply decorating your base, Bone Meal now helps you with these plants too.

Leads

You can now attach a lead to every mob of the game: passive mobs (villagers included), hostile mobs and aquatic mobs.

This makes it much easier to move mobs around: bring a villager to your trading hall, guide your animals to a new pen or keep a mob close to you while you work.

You are no longer limited to the few mobs that vanilla Minecraft allows you to leash.

String Duplication

String duplication is back and works the way it did before Minecraft 1.21.1.

Players who remember the old behavior can use it again as they did in earlier versions of the game, making it easier to produce large amounts of string for their farms and crafting.

Golden Dandelion

The Golden Dandelion has an enhanced utility on Cubesia: it lets you change the age of some mobs with a simple right-click.

  • Turns a baby into an adult (right-click).
  • Turns an adult into a baby (right-click).
  • Works on Villagers and all zombie variants.

Durability Ping

Durability Ping helps you avoid accidentally breaking your valuable equipment by warning you when it is close to breaking.

Whenever one of your equipped items falls below 10% of its maximum durability, you will automatically receive a notification reminding you to repair or replace it.

To prevent unnecessary spam, these warnings are protected by a cooldown, ensuring you are only notified occasionally instead of every time the item loses durability.

This quality-of-life feature gives you plenty of time to react, helping you protect your favorite gear and avoid costly accidents during mining, combat, or exploration.

Enchanted Book

Enchanted Books allow you to extract the enchantments from an item and convert them into individual enchanted books.

Simply place an enchanted item, such as a pickaxe, into the interface to view every enchantment it contains. You can then choose which enchantments you want to turn into books for later use or trading.

Once the process is completed, the selected enchantments are transferred onto enchanted books, while the original item is consumed and permanently removed.

This feature is especially useful for recovering valuable enchantments from powerful equipment, reorganizing your gear, or selling custom enchanted books to other players without having to rely entirely on the enchantment table.

Villager Reroll

Villager Reroll lets you exchange the trades of your villagers without having to break and place their job site block again.

Normally, getting a new set of offers means breaking the villager's workstation and placing it back, hoping for better trades. With Villager Reroll, you can do it directly on the villager, which saves time and keeps your trading hall untouched.

This makes it much easier to find the trade you are looking for, without having to rebuild or rearrange your villager setup every time.

No Too Expensive

The infamous "Too Expensive!" message of the anvil is gone: you can now combine, repair and rename items no matter how high the level cost is.

In vanilla Minecraft, an anvil refuses any operation that costs 40 levels or more. On Cubesia, that limit no longer exists, so you can keep improving your best equipment as much as you want.

You only need to have enough experience levels to pay for the operation.

Beyond Vanilla Levels

You can now go above the maximum levels of vanilla Minecraft: enchantments are no longer limited to their usual cap, such as Efficiency VI.

Combined with No Too Expensive, you can keep upgrading your favorite gear much further than in the base game.

The following vanilla enchantments can now reach a higher level on the anvil: Depth Strider V, Efficiency VIII, Feather Falling V, Fortune V, Frost Walker IV, Looting V, Luck of the Sea V, Lure VIII, Multishot V, Protection V, Respiration V, Soul Speed V, Sweeping Edge V, Thorns V and Unbreaking V.

You can check the maximum level of every custom enchantment in the Enchantments tables.
