## Welcome to Cubesia, a survival server where every detail has been crafted to make your adventure smoother, richer and more rewarding.

**⛏️ SURVIVE, BUILD, PROGRESS**

* Level up to 100 and unlock permanent bonuses, complete 88 collections, earn money with 13 jobs and discover 86 custom advancements. Pick a tag to show off your style, and climb the leaderboards by day, week, month or year.

**💰 A LIVING ECONOMY**

Trade on the Auction House, hunt rare offers on the hourly Black Market, post orders, set bounties or invest your money and let it work for you. Every item category has its own sell multiplier, and grades unlock more homes, vaults, auction slots and exclusive perks, with money or gems.

**🧟 SPAWNERS, MINIONS & FARMS**

Stack up to 10,000 spawners, store and filter their drops, and break them without Silk Touch. Put minions to work, harvest and replant with a single click, and keep lag away thanks to the item stacker and automatic cleanups.

**📚 80 CUSTOM ENCHANTMENTS**

Armor, bows, tools, fishing rods and weapons all have unique enchantments, and vanilla ones go beyond their usual limits. No more “Too Expensive” in the anvil, and you can even color your item names.

**🎣 FISHING, CRATES & EVENTS**

Catch 99 custom fish across seven rarities, enter fishing and mining competitions, open crates, vote for rewards, join chat games and enjoy seasonal events like our Halloween calendar.

**🛡️ QUALITY OF LIFE**

Carry animals and chests with CarryOn, open shulker boxes from your inventory, use portable workstations, graves to protect your loot, elevators, night vision, random teleport and so much more.

**💬 A COMMUNITY THAT STAYS CONNECTED**

Link your Minecraft account to Discord to chat with the server, receive your roles automatically and claim a welcome reward. Play with friends in teams, share chests and grow together.
